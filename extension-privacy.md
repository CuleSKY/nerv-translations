# NERV CS2ZE 浏览器扩展隐私说明

[English](#nerv-cs2ze-browser-extension-privacy-policy)

更新日期：2026-10-27

NERV CS2ZE 浏览器扩展不收集、不保存、不上传任何数据，不读取网页内容，不使用 Cookie 或分析工具。

扩展只做两件事：

- NERV CS2ZE 网站（cs2ze.org、cs2ze.org.cn、kepcs.cn 及其子域名）的页面连接 EXG 菜单地址 `wss://list.darkrp.cn:6514/Client/Ws` 时，把这一次 WebSocket 握手的 Origin 请求头改为 `http://localhost`；
- NERV CS2ZE 网站询问时，回答扩展的版本号。

你的 EXG 菜单密码由浏览器直接发给 EXG，不经过扩展。NERV CS2ZE 网站本身如何处理数据，以网站上的说明为准。

NERV CS2ZE 与 EXG 社区没有隶属关系，这个扩展未获 EXG 认可。

## NERV CS2ZE Browser Extension privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

The NERV CS2ZE browser extension collects, stores and uploads no data, reads no page content, and uses no cookies or analytics.

The extension does only two things:

- When a page of the NERV CS2ZE website (cs2ze.org, cs2ze.org.cn, kepcs.cn and their subdomains) connects to the EXG menu address `wss://list.darkrp.cn:6514/Client/Ws`, it sets the Origin header of that WebSocket handshake to `http://localhost`.
- When the NERV CS2ZE website asks, it answers with the extension's version number.

Your EXG menu password goes from your browser directly to EXG and does not pass through the extension. How the NERV CS2ZE website itself handles data is described on the website.

NERV CS2ZE is not affiliated with the EXG community, and this extension is not endorsed by EXG.
